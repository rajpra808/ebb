How it works

You don't have to open it. It tells you.

Install it once and forget about it. Ebb does the looking; you only make the call.

1

It scans every day

Quietly, when your Mac is idle and on power. It only reads sizes and dates; nothing is changed.

2

You get one notification

Only when at least 2 GB can go, and never more than once a day.

Questions

Fair questions.

Do I need to open the app every day?

No. Ebb scans by itself and sends a notification only when there's at least 2 GB to clear. Click it, check the panel, press Move to Trash. The full window is there when you want details.

Will it delete something I need?

It never deletes: items go to the Trash and every clean is logged, so you can put things back. Projects with any recent activity are skipped completely, and a never-touch list protects things like .git, .env and your AI tool settings.

Doesn't it all just come back?

Caches refill when a tool needs them, and Ebb says so for every item. Most of the space is from projects you stopped opening, which only comes back if you build them again.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

macOS hides parts of ~/Library and some tool folders without it. Ebb still works without it and tells you what it couldn't see.

Is it notarized?

Not yet. The install script downloads with curl, which macOS doesn't flag, and verifies the checksum and signature. If you download the zip in a browser, right-click Ebb and choose Open the first time.

How do I uninstall it?

curl -fsSL https://ebb.serveka.com/install.sh | sh -s -- --uninstall removes the app, the commands and the daily agent.
